## Research coverage limits (summary)

1. No authenticated portal session → could not see the live submission form, cannot confirm which fields are required, and could not confirm the Project point range.
2. Reward *weights* between rubric lines are not published anywhere I could find. The rubric is a pass/fail quality bar plus a discretionary 20–4,000 range, not a weighted score. Any numeric scoring in FINDINGS.md is my own stated method, not an official weight.
3. No judge interviews, no steward commentary on what actually passes. "Most submissions are rejected" is the only published base rate, plus the site-wide 18.5% acceptance rate (A11) across all contribution types.
4. Competitor set is deep on the security side (Immunefi, Sherlock, Forta, OZ) and shallow on the GenLayer side (organizer-authored lists only; I could not audit the Project Explorer).
5. No user was contacted. Payment evidence is inferred from published spend, not from a conversation with a budget owner.
